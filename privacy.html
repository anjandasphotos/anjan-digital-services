Privacy policy.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy describes how the Anjan Digital Services website is designed to handle information. The website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not provide a server-side enquiry form or an account login.

2. Enquiries through WhatsApp

When you click a WhatsApp link or submit the quick-enquiry form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. The website itself does not transmit that form content to a website backend. You choose whether to send the message. If you send it, WhatsApp and its applicable privacy terms govern its handling on that service.

3. Information you choose to share

Please share only what is needed to discuss your enquiry. Avoid sending passwords, one-time passcodes, full payment-card details or sensitive identity and financial documents in an initial message. If a document is needed for a service, ask how it will be used before sharing it.

4. Website hosting and external services

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as requests and logs according to the host’s policies. The website may load web fonts from Google Fonts; your browser may connect to that provider to retrieve them. You can consult the relevant providers’ current privacy policies for details.

5. Cookies and tracking

The site code does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Browser, hosting or third-party services may operate under their own policies.

6. Retention and document handling

This website does not receive or store documents through an upload form. If you choose to share documents with us through a separate communication channel during a service, the handling and retention depend on the specific task and applicable requirements. Please ask before sharing if you need clarification. We do not promise a fixed deletion period on this page.

7. Your choices and contact

You can choose not to submit a WhatsApp message or provide documents. For privacy questions about a service, contact us through WhatsApp and describe your concern without including sensitive information.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its practices change. Please review the date above when revisiting.

Note: This page is general website information and is not legal advice. Please review it against actual business practices before publishing.
