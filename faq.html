Frequently asked questions.

Important details about enquiries, documents, charges and how our assistance works.

Are you a government office or official website?
No. Anjan Digital Services is an independent assistance provider. Government departments and authorised service providers control their own systems, fees, eligibility rules and decisions.
How do I ask about a service and its charges?
Contact us on WhatsApp. We’ll discuss whether the service is available, what information is needed and the applicable service charges and official fees before work begins. Prices are not published on this website.
Can you guarantee approval or processing time?
No. We cannot guarantee approvals, appointments, ticket availability, processing times, visa outcomes, licence issuance, tax outcomes or loan approvals. These depend on the relevant authority, provider, eligibility and current availability.
Can I send my Aadhaar, PAN or bank documents on WhatsApp?
Please do not send sensitive identity, banking, tax or password information in your first message. First discuss what is required and why. Share documents only when necessary and after you understand the purpose and handling process.
Do you handle restricted or professional services directly?
Some services require an authorised provider, biometric verification, or a qualified professional. We provide only the assistance that is appropriate and available; restricted steps must be completed through the relevant authorised channel.
What about government fees and refunds?
Official charges are governed by the relevant authority or provider and may be non-refundable once paid or submitted. Our service-charge refund terms depend on whether work has started and are explained in the Terms & Conditions before proceeding.
Are the downloads official forms?
The downloads are general guides and checklists, unless a document itself clearly identifies a different source. Always check current official instructions and requirements with the relevant authority before applying.
Do you accept phone calls or email enquiries?
WhatsApp is the active contact channel shown on this website. Phone calling is Coming Soon. Please use WhatsApp for enquiries.
