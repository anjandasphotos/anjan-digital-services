About us

Practical support for digital life.

Anjan Digital Services is built around one simple idea: make everyday digital and documentation tasks easier to understand and navigate.

Who we are

Anjan Digital Services offers enquiry-based assistance with documentation, online forms, digital tasks, basic device troubleshooting and related services. We discuss the request first, confirm whether we can help, and explain the next steps before work begins.

Our approach

  • Clarity first: We aim to explain the service scope, documents and applicable charges before starting.
  • Customer review: Customers should carefully review all details before any application is submitted.
  • Responsible assistance: We do not claim to be a government office or promise approvals, processing times or financial outcomes.
  • Respect for privacy: Please share only the information needed for your enquiry. Avoid sending sensitive identity or financial documents until the need and process have been discussed.

Where we serve

Kolkata. Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm service availability.

Independent service notice: Government applications and decisions remain under the control of the relevant department or authorised service provider. Some tasks, including biometric Aadhaar updates and regulated professional work, can only be completed through authorised channels or qualified professionals.
